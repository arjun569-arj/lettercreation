Name

Export

The .3mf holds the letter, the name and each imported model as separate objects, lying flat and spaced apart. Badges and imported models are always separate objects. With the box above ticked, the surface design (such as the web) stays on the letter as a coloured part of it, and the name's back layer stays with the name.

Parts and filament

Badges

An image badge raises the dark or solid areas of a picture you own, such as your logo.

Figures and models

Download the file yourself from MakerWorld or another site, then add it here. Record its licence so the log shows what you may sell. Each model gets a matching socket cut into the letter, using the same clearance as the name slot, so it locates itself when you glue it. Rotation only poses the model; its part downloads in the original print orientation.

Selling prints of characters, logos or names owned by a studio needs a licence from the owner. Check the licence of every model, image and font you load.
